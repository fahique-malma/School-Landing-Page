# EAK International School — Admissions Landing Page

A responsive, frontend-only admissions landing page for EAK International School, created for the EakPustak Pvt. Ltd. — Frontend Web Development Intern, Second-Round Hiring Assignment.

The page is designed to help parents understand the school’s academic offering, explore its facilities, review sample admissions information, find answers to common questions, and reach an enquiry form.

## Features and required sections

1. Hero section — admissions announcement, academic session, value proposition, and calls to action.
2. Why choose EAK — six feature cards: Experienced Faculty, Strong Results, Modern Campus, Sports Culture, Safe Campus, and Technology-Enabled Learning.
3. Academic programs — Primary, Middle, Secondary, and Senior Secondary pathways, with grade ranges and descriptions.
4. School highlights — sample statistics and school highlights.
5. Facilities / campus gallery — visual cards for the campus, library, smart classrooms, science and computer labs, sports facilities and faculty, transport, medical support, and cafeteria.
6. Admission process — a clear, step-by-step overview from enquiry to enrolment.
7. Important dates — illustrative dates for key admission milestones.
8. Required documents — a checklist of typical documents families may need.
9. Fee information — sample annual and admission fees by grade range.
10. Parent testimonials — sample testimonials to demonstrate the layout.
11. Frequently asked questions — expandable FAQ accordion with plus/minus indicators.
12. Final call to action — actions to enquire, apply, or book a campus visit.

## Technology stack

- HTML5 — semantic page structure and content.
- CSS3 — layout, styling, responsive breakpoints, transitions, and animations.
- JavaScript — menu behavior, FAQ indicators, reveal animations, testimonial controls, and frontend form interactions.

## Run locally
1. Download and extract the project ZIP.
2. Open `index.html` in a modern browser.
3. An internet connection is needed for Google Fonts and the remote Unsplash photography.

## Included interactions
- Responsive navigation menu.
- Six “Why EAK” benefit cards.
- Card-style academic pathways for Primary, Middle, Secondary, and Senior Secondary.
- Campus gallery cards, including library, smart classrooms, science/computer labs, sports, transport, medical support, and cafeteria.
- FAQ.
- Scroll reveal animations, smooth anchor scrolling, testimonial controls, and a back-to-top button.
- Client-side enquiry form validation and demo confirmation.

## Important
This is a frontend-only demonstration. The enquiry form does not send or save data. School name, statistics, dates, fees, testimonials, and contact details are fictional/sample content and should be replaced with verified information before real-world publication. Images are from Unsplash.
