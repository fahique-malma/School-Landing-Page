Which grades are accepting applications? +

For this sample 2027–28 page, enquiries are welcome for Grades 1–12, subject to availability and the school’s admissions policy.

What does the admission process involve? +

The process includes an enquiry, application, appropriate assessment or interaction, a decision, and enrolment.

Which documents should we prepare? +

Please prepare the child’s birth certificate, previous school records where applicable, recent photographs, address proof, and parent or guardian ID.

Will my child need to take an entrance test? +

Depending on the grade, the school may arrange an appropriate assessment or interaction. The admissions team will share details after your enquiry.

How can we schedule a campus visit? +

Use the enquiry form below and choose “Book a campus visit.” This demo form shows a confirmation.
